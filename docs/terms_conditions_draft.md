## Service

MedVault provides tools for storing, organizing, exporting, and selectively sharing personal and family health records. Users are responsible for the accuracy of information they enter.

## Not Medical Advice

MedVault does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or emergency services. AI-generated content is informational and should be reviewed with a qualified healthcare professional.

## Emergency ID

Emergency ID displays user-selected health information for a care profile through a QR code or token when enabled. MedVault does not guarantee that emergency data is complete, current, or available in every situation.

## Doctor Packets and Share Links

Doctor Packets and share links include only the information selected by the user. Users are responsible for reviewing the selected scope before exporting or sharing.

## Subscriptions

MedVault Pro Family unlocks additional care profile, AI/OCR, caregiver dashboard, Doctor Packet, share link, and digest features. Basic access to user-owned records, basic Emergency ID, and basic user-selected export should not require Pro.

- India Pro: INR 299/month.
- International Pro target price: USD 9.99/month.

Play Store subscriptions are processed through Google Play Billing. Pro Family access may be removed if payment fails, a subscription is cancelled, a subscription expires, or payment verification fails.

## User Responsibilities

Users must keep account credentials secure, enter accurate information, and use family care profile features only for people whose records they are authorized to manage.

## Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their account and associated data. Some payment or legal records may be retained when required by law or payment processing rules.

## Limitation of Liability

MedVault is provided as a health record management tool. Users should not rely on the app as the sole source of medical or emergency information.
