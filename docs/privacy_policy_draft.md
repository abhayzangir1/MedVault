# MedVault Privacy Policy Draft

Effective date: To be set before Play Store submission.

MedVault helps users store and manage personal health records, including health events, medications, lab results, documents, symptoms, healthcare costs, family care profiles, and emergency information.

## Information We Collect

We collect account information such as name and email address. We collect health information that users choose to enter, including medical history, medications, lab results, symptoms, documents, receipts, allergies, chronic conditions, and emergency contacts.

We collect subscription information needed to manage Pro Family access, such as Google Play subscription identifiers, purchase tokens, and subscription status. We do not store full card numbers in MedVault.

## How We Use Information

We use information to provide app functionality, secure user accounts, store health records, generate user-selected Doctor Packets, show emergency information when enabled by the user, process subscriptions, and provide AI-assisted features when requested.

For export, AI analysis, Doctor Packet, doctor share links, Monthly Family Digest, and Emergency ID, users can choose which categories of health data are included. MedVault should use only the selected data for the selected purpose.

AI features are informational only and are not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

## Data Storage and Security

MedVault stores app data in Supabase. Data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections. Access controls are designed so users can access only their own records.

## Sharing

We do not sell user data. We share data only with service providers needed to operate the app, such as Supabase for backend storage and Google Play Billing for subscription processing.

Emergency ID information is shared publicly only when the user enables Emergency ID for a care profile and someone accesses that profile's emergency token or QR code. Doctor share links expose only the selected Data Packet scope until the link expires or is revoked.

## User Choices

Users can update profile information, disable Emergency ID, revoke share links, stop using AI features, cancel subscriptions through Google Play where applicable, and request deletion of their account and associated data.

## Children

MedVault is intended for users who are legally able to manage their own health records or manage family records with appropriate authority.
