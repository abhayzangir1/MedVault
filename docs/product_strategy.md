# MedVault Product Strategy

MedVault should compete as a family care and controlled health sharing app, not as a generic health record vault.

## Core Promise

Help families prepare for doctor visits, manage care across members, and share only the health data they choose.

## Differentiators

- Doctor Packet: appointment-ready summaries from selected data.
- Data Packet Builder: one consent layer for export, AI, Emergency ID, share links, and monthly digest.
- Caregiver Dashboard: family-wide view of meds, refills, labs, follow-ups, emergency readiness, and recent activity.
- Smart Import: OCR creates reviewable draft records instead of dead text.
- Scoped Share Links: time-limited doctor links without exposing the whole vault.

## Monetization

Basic access to user-owned health records, basic export, and basic Emergency ID stay free.

Pro Family charges for convenience and family complexity:

- up to 5 additional active care profiles,
- 100 AI/OCR uses/month,
- advanced Doctor Packets,
- family packets,
- share links with expiry,
- caregiver dashboard,
- monthly family digest,
- larger storage quota.

Future revenue can include medicine-ordering affiliates, insurance partners, diagnostics partners, or care-service referrals, but only after trust and data-control UX are strong.
